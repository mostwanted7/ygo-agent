## How to use this playbook

These are principles, not scripts: read the board, and when a better play exists, make it and say why in `why`. Trust the engine's options over this text.

## Game plan

Rank 8 Galaxy-Eyes toolbox: make Level 8 bodies in pairs and overlay them. Dragluxion is the hub: it searches, then becomes a 3000-ATK Rank 8 Dragon (38, 107 or Cipher Dragon) without costing a body. A good first turn ends on two Rank 8s with materials: a negater (38 vs Spells, 90 vs monster effects) next to 107, which lets Tachyon Transmigration be activated from hand. Grind with 90's search on their turn, Photon Emperor's revive and Primal's searches. Close with Cipher Dragon's steal, Full Armor/Cipher Blade removal and Afterglow's Battle Phase doubling.

## Card roles

**Level 8 makers**
- **Schwarzschild**: needs an empty field or their 2000+ ATK monster; brings itself + a Lv8 Dragon from Deck (effects negated), a Rank 8 alone. Hard 1/turn. Lock: only Dragon Xyz from the Extra Deck this turn, so **Number 90 (a Warrior) is off**. Pull Afterglow for its effect when detached from Dragluxion.
- **Galaxy Soldier**: discard a LIGHT → summons itself (Lv5), searches a Galaxy monster. Discard Emperor (chain its revive to the search) or Jumper.
- **Photon Emperor**: sent to the GY from anywhere but the field while another Photon/Galaxy is on your field/GY → revives in Defense (hard 1/turn). Summoned → one extra Normal Summon of a LIGHT: spend it on Wizard/Summoner, never on another Emperor (Lv8, needs 2 Tributes).
- **Galaxy Wizard**: Normal Summon and make it Lv8, or Tribute it to search; not both.
- **Galaxy Expedition**: with a Lv5+ Photon/Galaxy (Soldier, Afterglow) summons a Lv5+ from Deck; Primal is best (searches Transmigration).
- **Tachyon Primal**: Quick summon from hand while any Dragon Xyz is out; summoned (also from Deck) → searches Seventh Tachyon or Transmigration. Mandatory: attaches itself to any Dragon Xyz that declares an attack, **theirs included**.
- **Afterglow**: from hand while you control a Galaxy-Eyes (Primal counts). Detached as a cost → Galaxy-Eyes Photon Dragon from hand/Deck; in the Battle Phase it also doubles your Numbers. Put it under Dragluxion, 107 or C62.
- **Galaxy Summoner**: on summon revives a Photon/Galaxy (needs a free zone), and separately makes another LIGHT Lv4 (Soldier, Emperor) for Photon Blast.
- Locks: **Sanctuary/Delta Wing** LIGHT only; **Galaxy Trance** Photon/Galaxy only (Dragluxion and Schwarzschild are neither).

**Searchers**
- **Seventh Tachyon**: reveal 107 → any Lv8 LIGHT or Dragon (Schwarzschild, Afterglow), then top-deck your deadest card (e.g. Trance). **Seventh Ascension** only finds Seventh Tachyon. Galaxy-Eyes Photon Dragon (1 copy) comes from Afterglow, Vanisher, Seventh Tachyon, Orbital.
- **Galaxy Hundred**: on activation send Emperor (revives if another Galaxy is out) or Jumper. When Galaxy-Eyes Photon Dragon is Special Summoned, banish from their Extra Deck or summon one of their Numbers.
- **Orbital, Cleric, Jumper** (Jumper skips your next Battle Phase; GY → Spell/Trap search).

**Extra Deck and trap**
- **Dragluxion**: summoned → searches a Galaxy/Tachyon card (Primal; Hundred/Transmigration if Primal is in hand). Detach 2 → becomes 38, 107 or Cipher Dragon, then attach C107 (never makeable).
- **62 → C62**: ranks up for free, materials carry over. With Galaxy-Eyes Photon Dragon under it, C62 is unaffected by their monster effects and gains 100 × materials' Levels/Ranks.
- **Starliege Photon Blast** (Rank 4): summons a Photon from hand; their effects can't target/destroy your 2000+ ATK monsters, but not itself (1800), so expect it to eat removal. Still the home for leftover Lv4s.
- **Number 90**: negates a monster effect; on their turn searches/attaches a Photon/Galaxy card (use early, e.g. their Draw Phase); effect-indestructible with a Photon material.
- **38**: negates a Spell, redirects attacks; not Galaxy-Eyes. **107**: enables Transmigration from hand; at your Battle Phase start negates every other face-up monster, yours too.
- **Tachyon Transmigration**: needs a face-up Galaxy-Eyes (Cipher, 107, 62/C62, 90, Full Armor; not 38 or Dragluxion).
- **Cipher Dragon** steals; **Full Armor** destroys a face-up card; **Cipher Blade** destroys any card; **Cipher X** protects your LIGHTs.

## Situational considerations

- **Resolve Seventh Tachyon before Dragluxion becomes 107 or attaches C107**, because it must reveal a Number 101-107 in your Extra Deck and 107/C107 are your only ones; after that every Seventh card is dead. Spells don't break Schwarzschild's empty-field condition, so Ascension → Seventh Tachyon (add Afterglow #2 or Emperor; top-deck Sanctuary) can come first. If a Seventh Tachyon is still in hand/Deck and 107 has a material, decline the C107 attach.
- **Consider Dragluxion as the first Rank 8, turned into 107**, because it searches, its detach fires Afterglow, and 107 unlocks Transmigration from hand.
- **Do Schwarzschild before anything hits the field** (Seventh Tachyon fetches it); otherwise Soldier + Emperor gives the Lv8 pair.
- **Pick the second Rank 8 by their deck**: 90 vs monster effects (not on a Schwarzschild turn), 38 vs Spells, 62 → C62 when Galaxy-Eyes Photon Dragon is a free material.
- **Check locks before the first Extra Deck summon**: Schwarzschild, Trance, Sanctuary/Delta Wing, Seventh Tachyon (Xyz only).
- **Overlay before summoning more** near five monsters; Summoner's revive fails with no free zone.
- **Spend Transmigration from hand on their starter**; search the 2nd copy with Primal or Dragluxion.

## Lethal check

- **Attackers**: every Rank 8 still makeable. Number 62 deals half damage unless Galaxy-Eyes Photon Dragon is a material; in damage calculation it gains 200 × total Ranks of all Xyz on the field (once per battle, detach 1).
- **Afterglow doubling**: detach it in the Battle Phase (e.g. 107's or C62's cost at its start): Numbers double and Galaxy-Eyes Photon Dragon arrives as an extra 3000 attacker. 107 negating your own monsters can switch off 62's halving and C62's extra attacks.
- **Blockers**: Cipher Dragon steals one (only it attacks directly that turn), Full Armor/Cipher Blade destroy one, C62 attacks monsters 3 times, S38 can detach to become 1500 and attack directly.
- **Attack with Primal before any Dragon Xyz**, since it attaches to the first one that attacks.
- Worked example: 107 (3000) + 62 with Galaxy-Eyes Photon Dragon as material (4000, +3200 from 2×Rank 8) = 10200 into an empty field.

## Going second / breaking boards

- Schwarzschild is live against a 2000+ ATK monster even if your field isn't empty.
- Remove, then attack: Cipher Dragon takes their best monster, Cipher Blade on it destroys another, Full Armor onto another Galaxy-Eyes Xyz, S38 handles backrow.
- Against a negate, lead with the cheaper threat (Dragluxion's search) before the key Xyz.
- If you can't break through, build Number 90 + Transmigration and wait.

## Example sequences

1. **Schwarzschild**: pull Afterglow → Dragluxion (search Primal) → Primal (search Transmigration) → Dragluxion to 107; Afterglow → Photon Dragon; it + Primal → 62 → C62. Soldier → Summoner revives Afterglow, Soldier Lv4 → Photon Blast.
2. **Soldier + Emperor**: discard Emperor (revives; search Wizard) → extra NS Wizard Lv8 → Dragluxion (Primal) → Primal → Seventh Tachyon (before 107) → Afterglow → 107; Summoner revives Emperor, Soldier Lv4 → Photon Blast; Afterglow + Emperor → 90.
3. **Seventh Tachyon → Schwarzschild**: pull Afterglow → Expedition: Primal (Transmigration) → Afterglow → Dragluxion (2nd Transmigration) → 107; Photon Dragon + Wizard → 62 → C62; Primal + Afterglow → 38.
