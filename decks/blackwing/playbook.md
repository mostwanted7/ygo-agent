## How to use this playbook

These are principles, not scripts: read the board and adapt lines to your cards, zones and locks. Trust the engine's options over this text, and check the Deck and Extra Deck before committing to a line that needs a particular card.

## Game plan

Synchro combo. Turn Simoon (or another starter) into chained free summons and searches, ending on **Blackwing Full Armor Master** (unaffected by other cards, steals monsters) + **Black-Winged Assault Dragon** (700 burn per opponent monster effect; field wipe), backed by **Black Feather Whirlwind** (revives, protects) and Set traps.

## How the engine works (the rules that make the combos)

- **Simoon** (key starter): needs no monsters on your field and another Blackwing in hand to banish; places Black Whirlwind from the Deck, then Normal Summons itself without using your normal Normal Summon. Locks you to DARK Extra Deck monsters this turn (all Blackwing Synchros are DARK; Clear Wing, Black Rose, PSY-Framelord Omega, Coral Dragon are not). End Phase: Black Whirlwind goes to GY, 1000 damage.
- **Black Whirlwind**: every Blackwing **Normal** Summon (not Special) adds a lower-ATK Blackwing; no once-per-turn. Simoon 1600 → Sudri 1400 → Vata 800.
- **Sudri**: Normal Summon adds a card mentioning "Black-Winged Dragon" (Shamal, Vata, Chinook, Twin Shadow, Blackbird Close…). Tribute 1 monster → Phantom Glimmer Token (Lv2 DARK Tuner), 700 damage, Synchro-only Extra Deck that turn.
- **Shamal**: send from hand/field → place Black Feather Whirlwind from the Deck. In GY, when a Blackwing Synchro or BWD is Special Summoned to your field: banish it → add a Blackwing from GY (700 damage).
- **Vata**: Special Summon from hand with another Blackwing. Send Vata + non-Tuner Blackwings **from the Deck** totalling Level 8 → Special Summon BWD (DARK-only lock). Sends: Kris 4 + Chinook 2, Bora 4 + Harmattan 2, or Simoon 6; pick what Shamal or Black Feather Whirlwind will want in the GY.
- **Black Feather Whirlwind**: once per turn, when you Special Summon a DARK Synchro from the Extra Deck (incl. BWD via Vata), revive a lower-ATK Blackwing/BWD from GY or banishment. Also protects DARK monsters from destruction by removing a Black Feather Counter.
- **Black-Winged Dragon (BWD)**: effect damage to you becomes counters on it, so do self-damage (Shamal 700, Sudri 700, Simoon's End Phase 1000) while it's out; later spend counters to shrink and burn.
- **Boreastorm** (Lv6 DARK Tuner Synchro; DARK Tuner + non-Tuner): on Synchro Summon, send a Blackwing from the Deck; its Level becomes that card's, tuning it for the next Synchro.
- **Assault Dragon** (Lv10): Special Summon from the Extra Deck by banishing a Tuner Synchro (Boreastorm, Coral, or a Gofu-revived Synchro) + BWD from field and/or GY; no Synchro Summon needed.
- **Full Armor Master** (Lv10): Blackwing Tuner + non-Tuners totalling 10: Boreastorm Lv2 + BWD, or Boreastorm Lv4 + Simoon.
- **Extenders** (Special Summon from hand with another Blackwing): Kris Lv4 (once per turn), Bora Lv4, Gale Lv3 Tuner (halves ATK/DEF), Harmattan Lv2 (can add another Blackwing's Level), Vata Lv2 Tuner, Oroshi Lv1 Tuner. Sharnga (Lv2 Tuner) needs a 2000+ ATK monster on the field.
- **Gofu** (Lv5 Tuner): Special Summon only with no monsters (competes with Simoon). Its 2 tokens can't be Tributed or Synchro Material and clog zones, but count for Gofu's banish: banish Gofu + non-Tuners → revive a Blackwing Synchro of that total Level as a Tuner.
- **Vayu** (GY): banish it + a non-Tuner Blackwing from GY → Blackwing Synchro of combined Level, effects negated. **Twin Shadow** (from hand with 2+ Blackwings): shuffle a Tuner + non-Tuner Blackwing from GY/banished → Blackwing Synchro or BWD of that Level (counts as Synchro Summon).
- **Ahrima**: discard → add Lair of Darkness. Tribute another DARK → add **Simoon** (the only DARK 2000+ DEF in the Deck). With **Lair**, all monsters are DARK and once per turn an effect Tribute can be an opponent's monster, turning Ahrima, Sudri, Icarus Attack and Enemy Controller into removal.
- **Chinook**: send from hand/field → send a Blackwing Synchro or BWD from the Extra Deck to GY; an opponent's monster -700 and negated. Quick Effect while you control a DARK Synchro. Also puts BWD in GY for Assault Dragon.

## Main line: Simoon + any other Blackwing

Typical route: Simoon (banish the least useful Blackwing) → Whirlwind adds Sudri → Normal Summon Sudri (adds Shamal; Whirlwind adds Vata) → send Shamal (Black Feather Whirlwind) → Vata → BWD → Black Feather Whirlwind revives a Blackwing under 2800 ATK; Shamal returns a Blackwing (700 → counter) → Sudri token if you need a Tuner → Boreastorm (e.g. token 2 + Sudri 4), set to Lv2 (Chinook/Harmattan) or Lv4 (Kris/Bora/Sudri) → Full Armor Master → Assault Dragon (banish Boreastorm + BWD) → Set traps (Twin Shadow, Icarus Attack, Blackbird Close, Delta Crow).
- A second Black Whirlwind: activate it before Normal Summoning Sudri for an extra search.
- Spare materials: Harmattan copying a Lv4 Boreastorm becomes Lv6; with Oroshi → Armor Master, only if materials and zones for Full Armor Master and Assault Dragon remain.
- Before each step, check the 5 Main Monster Zones, what's left in the Deck, and the next Synchro's Levels and Tuner.

## Other starts

- **Ahrima** without Simoon: Tribute another DARK → add Simoon; Simoon still needs an empty board, so plan around it.
- **Sudri** as Normal Summon: add Vata → Vata → BWD.
- **Gofu + Bora + Gale + Oroshi** on an empty board: Gofu (tokens) → Bora, Gale → Armor Master (3+4) → Oroshi → BWD (1+7) → Gofu banishes itself + tokens (5+1+1 = 7) to revive Armor Master as a Tuner → Assault Dragon (Armor Master + BWD). Without Bora/Gale: Gofu 5 + Harmattan 2 = Lv7 Armor Master.

## Going second / breaking boards

- **Dark Armed Dragon**: Special Summon with exactly 3 DARK in GY; banish a DARK from GY to destroy any card, no once-per-turn.
- **Delta Crow** (from hand with exactly 3 Blackwings) destroys all their face-down Spells/Traps; **Icarus Attack** destroys 2; **MST** and **Enemy Controller** (position change, or Tribute → take control) clear the rest.
- Gale halves ATK/DEF, Chinook -700 and negates, Nothung's summon burns 800 and gives -800.
- Black Rose wipes the field, only on a turn without Simoon or Vata (it's FIRE).

## Situational considerations

- **Consider Assault Dragon's wipe** against removal, a major threat or a board you can't beat in battle; Full Armor Master survives it (unaffected). It works only on the opponent's turn; their End Phase is the last chance to clear blockers before your turn.
- **Use Chinook after an attack is declared** when its -700 or negation wins the battle.
- **Keep Blackbird Close in hand while you control a Blackwing Synchro**: it can activate from hand then and dodges Spell/Trap removal.
- **Spend protection by threat**: Black Feather Whirlwind's counters for useful bodies; Chinook and Blackbird Close for threats that would break your board.
- **Keep a spare Blackwing for Simoon** when you expect your board cleared.
- **Gofu without a searcher**: Armor Master + BWD when two bodies or Delta Crow matter; Assault Dragon against decks that activate many monster effects.

## Locks and traps to respect

- **Blackbird Close** only negates effects activated by an opponent's monster on the field (not hand/GY), so it can't stop Honest; its cost is a face-up Blackwing you send to GY. Full Armor Master's immunity doesn't stop Honest boosting its attacker. Prefer stopping a starter that enables a lock before it resolves (Tour Guide into Djinn Releaser).
- Simoon or Vata used → DARK-only Extra Deck; Sudri's Tribute → Synchro-only Extra Deck.
- Gofu and Simoon each need an empty board: decide which starts. Gofu tokens and extenders fill the 5 zones fast.
- **Only one BWD in the Extra Deck.** Once it leaves, check that Vata, Twin Shadow or Blackbird Close still has a valid target before committing.
- **BWD and Assault Dragon are not Blackwings**: they don't enable extenders or count for Delta Crow.

## Known pitfalls

- **Let March of the Monarchs resolve when the opponent controls no Tribute Summoned monster**: it only stops targeting and destruction of Tribute Summoned monsters. A Set Icarus Attack can chain later and destroy March along with the monster it protects or another live card.
