# Tips for Geargia

The deck owner's advice, in their own words. The trainer writes the playbook from it and `ygo-agent learn` folds new tips in. Edit freely.

## Gold Gadget with Geargiarmor or Geargiarsenal (2026-10-22)

With Gold Gadget and Geargiarmor (or Geargiarsenal, which can Tribute itself into Armor) in hand, I'd rather end on Gold Gadget plus a face-down Armor than make Gear Gigant X. Both replace themselves when they leave (the Gadget floats into Silver Gadget, a flipped Armor searches), so I keep my card advantage and the opponent has to get through layered protection.

## When Gear Gigant X is worth it (2026-10-22)

Gear Gigant X is worth making when a Geargiano (or Geargiano Mk-II) is already in the GY: when it leaves the field it brings one back, so it doesn't cost me a body. Without one there, two bodies that replace themselves are usually better.

## Geargiagear timing (2026-10-22)

Use Geargiagear at the end of the opponent's turn, so the two Geargianos it summons are fresh for my turn as Xyz Material or Tributes, and the opponent never gets a turn to deal with them.

## Look for the OTK (2026-10-22)

Look for non-obvious OTKs: one or two Limiter Removals on my Machines (a second one doubles again), with Gagaga Cowboy in Defense Position as the finisher for the last 800. Count it before settling for a normal turn.
