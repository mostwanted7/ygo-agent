# Tips for domain-monarchs

The deck owner's advice, in their own words. The trainer writes the playbook from it and `ygo-agent learn` folds new tips in. Edit freely.

## Going first, what end board are you aiming for (for example, Tribute Summoned Ehther or Erebus behind Domain, with Escalation or Ehther's hand effect to Tribute Summon again on the opponent's turn), a (2026-10-22)

You want to tribute summon one of the monarchs. All of them are decent going first except for Caius, because caius needs a card to banish and your opponent does not have a card when you are going first. You want to use your spells to get the 2 pieces for a tribute summon if you are going first. You need a monster to tribute + a monarch in your hand. Use pantheism and/or tenacity to maximize your chances for that, if you didn't draw perfect starter already. Then just lock the opponent as much as you want, either with Domain, Erupt, March, or maybe set up a stormforth/escalation so you can disrupt them on their turn. Remember to banish cards that you want to return to your hand later with edea effect.

## Which opening hands do you count as real starters (Edea, Eidos, Mithra, Tenacity plus a Monarch, Domain plus a Lv8), and which one do you play first when you hold more than one? (2026-10-22)

If I expect distruption, i try to go for domain first and not use my normal summon. So mithra, landrobe or similar. or I am not expecting disruption, edea into eidos is generally really good for card advantage

## When Tenacity, Pantheism's GY effect, or Erebus/Ehther's 'send 2 Monarch Spells/Traps' cost lets you pick Monarch Spells/Traps, what's your usual priority (Domain, Stormforth, Return, Prime Monarch, E (2026-10-22)

Pantheism and Prime Monarch are the only S/T that have effects in graveyard, so I try to have at least one of each there at all times.

## When you Tribute Summon a Lv8, how do you choose between Erebus (shuffle away a card, recurse from GY), Ehther (Special Summon a 2400/1000 from Deck), Thestalos, and Domain's level reduction to Normal (2026-10-22)

Situational. Thestalos is obviously really good on your first turn when you are going first and opponent has no board yet. Majestys fiend is going to be good when opponent is relying on effects and cant just run you over. Especially good if you also get march out. You normally never want to tribute summon Kuraz (unless no other options), but ss him with Ehther instead, and I often use him to generate card advantage by popping my own cards (including Kuraz), but you can of course also pop enemy cards if they are so problematic.

Extremely situational.

## Anything else the agent should know about this deck? (2026-10-22)

During opponents turn, you might sometimes have to activate stormforth before activating Ehther from hand, because you dont have any other monarch S/Ts in the graveyard to activate ehther in the first place. THis goes against the stanard chain you might want, but its simply necessary sometimes.
