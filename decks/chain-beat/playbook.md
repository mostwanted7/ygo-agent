## How to use this playbook

These are principles, not scripts: read the board, and when a better play exists, make it and say why in `why`. Trust the engine's options over this text.

## Game plan

Trap-heavy control with "floating" Level 3-4 beaters. Prefer going first. Your monsters (Wind-Up Rabbit, Evilswarm Thunderbird, Fairy Tail - Luna) can leave the field at instant speed and come back, so your Dark Hole and Torrential hurt only the opponent. Behind them sit Solemn Strike, Torrential Tribute, Macro Cosmos and a Set Waking the Dragon. Black Garden halves every monster they summon. Yata-Garasu, Drowning Mirror Force and Raidraptor - Ultimate Falcon close the game. A good first turn: draw spells, one monster, Black Garden, then every trap Set. Your own Garden, Torrential and Dark Hole can hurt you, so check your side before each one.

## Card roles

- **Wind-Up Rabbit**: its Quick Effect banishes a face-up Wind-Up until your next Standby Phase. It needs a **face-up** target, so a Set Rabbit can't dodge. It works once while face-up, but it comes back as a fresh card (NO_TURN_RESET count). If you use it on your turn, it misses their whole turn, so prefer their turn, in response to removal or an attack.
- **Evilswarm Thunderbird**: responds to **any** activation, yours too (your Garden activation, Yata's End Phase return). Hard once per turn. It returns at the **next** Standby Phase with +300. The bonus is lost when it leaves the field, so it never goes above 1950. If you banish it on your turn, it is back for their Standby with its effect ready.
- **The return is not a Summon**: Garden doesn't halve it, Torrential and Solemn can't hit it, and any halving it had before is wiped.
- **Fairy Tail - Luna**: when Normal Summoned, it searches the other Luna. Its Quick Effect returns itself and one face-up opponent's monster to the hand, once per turn per copy. They can negate it by sending a same-name copy from their Deck or Extra Deck to the GY. Macro Cosmos stops that. Re-summon Luna to search again.
- **Yata-Garasu**: returns to your hand each End Phase. If it deals battle damage, they skip their next draw. It locks an empty field, but it costs your Normal Summon every turn.
- **Black Garden**: triggers only on Normal/Special Summon **success**. A Set or a Flip Summon gets no halving and makes no token. Whoever summons is halved, and the **other** player gets an 800 Rose Token. Falcon is immune.
- **Pot of Duality**: no Special Summon that turn, before or after it (a Rose Token you give them counts). Play it before your Normal Summon.
- **Pot of Desires**: needs 12+ cards in your Deck.
- **Waking the Dragon**: Set it as bait; it replaces backrow protection. If an opponent's effect destroys or banishes it while face-down, summon **Ultimate Falcon**, which is the right pick almost every time. Falcon has 3500 ATK and is unaffected by other cards, including your own Dark Hole and Torrential, and it can't take Moon Mirror Shield. Your own MST on Waking does nothing, and a Duality turn blocks it.
- **Falcon's limits**: Waking summons it with no Xyz materials, so neither its detach effect nor its End Phase effect (requires a Raidraptor material) is available. Synchro Zone restricts both players' attack declarations: Falcon is an Xyz, so it cannot attack through Zone despite its immunity. Remove/negate Zone before counting Falcon's battle damage; immunity alone does not break this stall.
- **Solemn Strike**: hits Special Summons and monster effects only. Costs 1500 LP each time.
- **Drowning Mirror Force**: **direct** attacks only. Shuffles every Attack Position monster they have into the Deck.
- **Macro Cosmos**: very strong. It banishes everything bound for a GY, yours too, and stops Luna's negation and GY plays. Ignore the Helios option.
- **Moon Mirror Shield**: in battle with a face-up monster, the equipped monster's ATK becomes that monster's higher stat +100. Put it on whatever fits, even a token. If face-up Shield goes from field to GY (e.g. its monster bounces), its 500-LP recycling trigger is mandatory; choose top or bottom of the Deck. Account for the LP payment and next draw. It needs a free Spell/Trap Zone.
- **Forbidden Chalice**: negates a monster's effects and gives +400, even in the Damage Step. Save it for their boss. On Yata it cancels the draw skip.
- **Rank 4s**: last resort, because they cost you cards and you lose the floating effects. Castel spins a card, Tornado Dragon pops backrow, Dark Rebellion halves a boss, Gagaga Cowboy burns 800, Abyss Dweller locks their GY.

## Situational considerations

- **Set every trap going first**, because the deck is reactive. Hold Dark Hole until they commit and the Shield until a battle. Two Wakings plus four other cards fill all five zones, so leave one zone free if you can.
- **Play Pot of Duality almost every turn, before the Normal Summon**, unless you are sure you need Waking that turn. Take the missing piece: a monster if you have none, then Black Garden (or Terraforming), then the best trap. Play Upstart first to see more. Always play the first Desires, and skip the second only if deck-out is near.
- **Summon first, then Black Garden**, because Garden taxes your own summons: it halves your monster and gives them an 800 blocker. If you are the one summoning each turn to close the game, hold Garden and play it when they start summoning again.
- **No monster to summon**: Set your traps and leave the field empty. If Drowning is Set, hold Garden, because the Rose Tokens it gives you stop their direct attacks. Without Drowning, play Garden: each of their Extra Deck summons gives you a token that blocks, soaks removal and attacks for 800.
- **Keep a Monster Zone free**: with all five full, Garden gives no tokens and you can't Normal Summon.
- **Summon Rabbit, Thunderbird and Luna face-up**, even under Garden. Set, they can't use their effects and die to your own Torrential.
- **Wipe with Dark Hole or Torrential once they have committed several monsters or a boss**, after checking that your monsters can dodge.
- **Bait negators that pay a cost** (Stardust Dragon/Assault Mode Tribute themselves to negate). Open with your cheapest activation (Terraforming, an Equip, a Field Spell), then attack the empty field. The Shield's boost is an activation at damage calculation, so it can be negated. Equip it while the negator is gone, and attack first with the Shield monster.
- **Use Luna on their best monster when it matters.** Under Macro Cosmos it is a sure answer, so flip Macro Cosmos early against GY decks and before you rely on Luna.
- **Don't waste Rabbit's banish when nothing threatens.** Thunderbird's End Phase banish is free (chain it to Yata's return or to any activation of theirs): it comes back at their Standby at 1950 with its effect ready. Rabbit can only target Wind-Ups. To save Thunderbird from an attack when there is nothing to chain to, Rabbit can banish itself to create an activation.
- **Close an empty field with one beater, then Yata**: spend one Normal Summon on your best beater, then attack with Yata plus the beater every turn. Yata alone (200 a turn) loses to deck-out.
- **Attack with tokens first into Set backrow**, so their attack traps hit an 800 token.

## Lethal check

- **Attackers**: Luna 1850, Thunderbird 1650 (1950 after a return), Rabbit 1400, Yata 200, Falcon 3500, Rose Tokens 800. A monster you Normal Summon under a face-up Black Garden is halved and gives them an 800 token that blocks one attack.
- **Boosts**: Chalice +400. Moon Mirror Shield wins one battle. Gagaga Cowboy burns 800.
- **Removing blockers**: Dark Hole, Luna's bounce, Castel. A monster banished to dodge a wipe stays gone until the next Standby, so count only what stays on the field.
- **Upstart Goblin gives them 1000 LP**, so don't play it in a lethal turn.
- **Skip a Normal Summon that only adds a blocker**: under Black Garden, attack first with what is on the field, then summon in Main Phase 2.

## Going second / breaking boards

- First, MST or Tornado Dragon on their key backrow. If Waking is still Set, let it bait.
- Then wipe their monsters: Dark Hole, or Luna's bounce on a single boss. Then summon and attack. With their field empty, Yata attacks first.
- If you can't break the board, build instead: Duality, one monster, Black Garden, Set traps, and let them come to you.

## Example sequences

- **Luna opener**: (Pot of Duality) → Normal Summon Luna, search Luna → Black Garden → Set Solemn Strike, Waking and the other traps.
- **Draw then set**: Pot of Desires → Upstart → Duality → Normal Summon the best monster (Luna > Thunderbird > Rabbit) → Black Garden if drawn → Set Torrential, Solemn, Macro Cosmos.
- **Yata lock going second**: MST a Set card → Dark Hole → Normal Summon Yata, attack directly → MP2: Set Solemn, Black Garden → Yata returns in the End Phase.
