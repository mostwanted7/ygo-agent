# Tips for chain-beat

The deck owner's advice, in their own words. The trainer writes the playbook from it and `ygo-agent learn` folds new tips in. Edit freely.

## What is the deck's main way to win? Is it a Black Garden field where Wind-Up Rabbit and Evilswarm Thunderbird keep banishing themselves and coming back (not a Summon, so their ATK isn't halved), while (2026-10-22)

Deck is extremely versatile, you win by controlling the opponent, multiple win conditions. Your cards can also disrupt your own plays, so be careful. I suggest activating Black garden after you have summoned a monster, if possible. But all your monsters have a "bounce" effect that can dodge a lot of danger, including the black garden effect.

## When Waking the Dragon goes off, do you always summon Raidraptor - Ultimate Falcon, or do you sometimes pick something else (a Rank 4, or a main-deck monster)? And do you set Waking just to bait the o (2026-10-22)

I always set it to bait backrow removal yes, I do this rather than run backrow protection. Ultimate falcon is the obvious optimal choice in 99.99% of cases.

## Would you rather go first or second, and how does your first turn change between the two? For example, how many traps do you set versus how many draw spells do you burn before setting? (2026-10-22)

You are playing a trap heavy deck, going first makes more sense. I don't see a reason NOT to set traps. Spells you will sometimes want to hold, depending on what they are.

## When do you activate Dark Hole or Torrential Tribute while you have your own monsters out? Do you dodge with Rabbit or Thunderbird first, and do you hold them until the opponent commits several monste (2026-10-22)

You try to maximize their value ofcourse, but this depends heavily on what the opponent is doing/playing, you cant/wont always have the sufficient knowledge to make the best decision possible. Use the available information to determine the best timing, and of course dodge the effect with thunderbird/rabbit/luna if possible.

## Since Pot of Duality stops you from Special Summoning that turn, when do you play it, and what do you usually take off it (Black Garden, a trap, a monster)? Also, when should you skip Pot of Desires b (2026-10-22)

Deck has no special summons (besides black garden effect), so always activate pot of duality unless you are certain you will need Waking the Dragon that turn (highly unlikely). I only skip Pot of Desires if I expect to deck out quickly after using it. There are 2 copies, the first one I activate always.

## Which monster usually gets Moon Mirror Shield? And when do you make a Rank 4 (which one first) instead of keeping your Level 4s on the field as separate bodies? (2026-10-22)

Rank 4s are your last option since they put you at a card disadvantage by default, and you lose the floating effects. Moon Mirror shield is situational, sometimes even on a token if thats what you have to do. Keep in mind that it goes straight to the GY if your monster bounces (like wind up rabbit or thunderbird)

## Anything else the agent should know about this deck? (2026-10-22)

Keep in mind that your macro cosmos is very powerful card. It prevents opponents from negating Lunas effect, among other things.
