## Coaching lessons

1. **Search priority.** To summon Saffira you need two things: Saffira, Queen of Dragons, and a way to Ritual
   Summon her (Hymn of Light, Prayers, or SDQ's GY effect). Search whichever is missing. If you already have
   both, search a **Cyber Angel** to use as Tribute fodder with Manju or Preparation of Rites, NOT a second Saffira.
   Prefer Benten: when Tributed it searches a LIGHT Fairy, so it replaces itself.
2. **Ritual Spells belong in the GY.** From there they protect Saffira: Hymn stops destruction, Prayers brings
   a replacement Saffira. Prefer lines that end with them in the GY (SDQ's discard sends one; using one to
   summon puts it there).
3. **Don't waste bodies on Tributes.** Only Tribute cards that gain something from it: a Cyber Angel (Benten
   searches a LIGHT Fairy, Idaten gives your Ritual monsters +1000 ATK/DEF), or Tour Guide plus the Djinn it
   summoned (the Djinn gives Saffira its effect). Never Tribute hand traps or monsters you have another use for.
4. **Saffira's End Phase effect recycles hand traps.** If a hand trap that's good against this opponent is in
   your GY (Effect Veiler, Ghost Ogre), add it back with the "add 1 LIGHT monster from GY" mode. Otherwise
   draw 2 and discard 1 to dig for one. Pick hand traps for the matchup: Veiler against monster effects in
   their Main Phase, Ghost Ogre against effects of cards already on the field.
5. **Use Tour Guide's Xyz against problem cards.** If the opponent relies on Set cards or has another annoying
   card on the field, make a Rank 3 with Tour Guide + its Lv3 Fiend (Ghostrick Alucard destroys a Set card)
   instead of using them as Tributes.
6. **Synchro is a last resort.** The Tuners are your hand traps (Veiler, Ghost Ogre), so Synchro Summoning
   spends them. Tour Guide's Fiend can't be Synchro Material anyway.
7. **Remember how Saffira was summoned.** If Djinn Demolisher was one of the Tributes, the opponent can't
   target her, so holding Sauravis for protection is redundant; treat it as a spare card (e.g. Saffira's
   draw-2 discard). With Djinn Releaser the opponent can't Special Summon, so Sauravis's Summon negation is
   redundant too. Neither Djinn stops non-targeting removal; Hymn/Prayers in the GY cover that.

## Game plan

Ritual midrange. Ritual Summon **Saffira, Queen of Dragons** (Lv6, 2500/2400) without spending real cards,
protect her with Ritual Spells in the GY, a Djinn and hand traps, and use her End Phase effect every turn to
keep hand traps flowing. Grind the opponent out; Honest makes any LIGHT monster win a battle. These are
principles, not scripts: read the board, deviate when a better play exists and say why, and trust the
engine's options over the text.

## Engine cards

- **SDQ**: two separate once-per-turn effects. Hand: discard it → send a Ritual Spell from Deck to GY, then
  you *may* add Saffira or Sauravis from Deck/GY. Send **Hymn** by default, Prayers if Hymn is already in the
  GY or their removal banishes/bounces. GY: banish it → Ritual Summon Saffira/Sauravis from hand, no Ritual
  Spell needed. So SDQ is both the summon and the Saffira search; you only need Tribute fodder.
- **Count Tribute Levels before picking the Ritual monster** (SDQ, Prayers): Saffira is 6 (one Lv6 or 3+3).
  Sauravis is 7, so a single Lv6 isn't enough; check whether the extra Tribute would consume a body you need.
  When both Ritual monsters are available, consider summoning Saffira with Sauravis as its Tribute.
- **Saffira**: End Phase, if Ritual Summoned this turn OR a LIGHT monster went from hand/Deck to GY while
  she was face-up; one mode per turn (lesson 4).
- **Hymn** in GY: banish it instead of your Ritual monster being destroyed by battle or effect.
- **Prayers**: Tributes only LIGHT. **Target Saffira** when you can: a fresh Ritual Summon gives her End Phase
  effect, and Sauravis in hand alone covers her Level. In GY: when your face-up LIGHT Ritual monster leaves
  the field by an opponent's card effect, banish it → Special Summon Saffira/Sauravis from hand/Deck (that
  Saffira isn't Ritual Summoned).
- **Pre-Preparation** (1/turn: Hymn + Saffira, or Prayers + Saffira/Sauravis) when you have neither half.
  **Preparation of Rites**: Saffira if missing, else a Cyber Angel, plus a Ritual Spell from GY if any.
- **Tour Guide**: Djinn (or Tour Guide) from Deck, effects negated; 3+3 = Saffira's Tributes, or Rank 3.
- **Djinns** as Ritual Tribute (from hand, field, or banished from GY), even if Tour Guide negated them:
  Releaser = **opponent can't Special Summon** while the Ritual monster is face-up; Demolisher = **can't be
  targeted** by opponent's effects. Releaser vs heavy Special Summoners, Demolisher vs targeting removal.
- **Benten**: Lv6, covers Saffira alone; Tributed → add a LIGHT Fairy (Honest, Manju). **Idaten**: Tributed →
  your Ritual monsters +1000 ATK/DEF; Ritual Summoned (only via Prayers) → Ritual Spell from Deck/GY.
- **Sauravis**: keep as hand protection or use as Saffira's Tribute when those roles matter more than its
  summon negation. Before Ritual Summoning it, count the Lv7 Tribute cost and compare with summoning Saffira
  for her End Phase effect. Check whether a Djinn already covers the protection you need (lesson 7).

## Support cards

- **Honest**: in the Damage Step your battling LIGHT monster gains the opposing monster's ATK; in Main Phase,
  return face-up Honest to hand.
- **Condemned Witch**: Normal Summon → Forbidden Lance; opponent's Main Phase, Tribute it → Lv4 Fairy from
  Deck (Honest, Manju).
- **Veiler / Ghost Ogre**: save for their key starter or boss; Saffira gets them back (lesson 4).
- **Change of Heart**: take their best monster, then attack with it or use it as Tribute/Xyz material so it
  never goes back.
- **Forbidden Lance**: saves Saffira from a Spell/Trap or wins a battle. Set it before ending your turn to
  use it during the opponent's turn; keep it in hand only when you intend to use it this turn.

## Extra Deck

- Rank 3 (Tour Guide + Djinn/Tour Guide): **Ghostrick Alucard** (destroy a Set card), Wind-Up Zenmaines. The
  main backrow answer (lesson 5).
- Rank 4 (Manju, Honest, Witch): Castel (flip a monster face-down / shuffle a card into the Deck), Dark
  Rebellion, Gagaga Cowboy (800 damage in Defense), Abyss Dweller.
- Rank 6 (Saffira + a Cyber Angel): Ptolemy M7 (return a monster on field or in either GY to hand),
  Beatrice (send any card from Deck to GY), Jinzo - Layered (take control of a monster).
- Synchro 7, last resort (lesson 6): Black Rose Dragon (destroys ALL cards on the field when Synchro
  Summoned), Clear Wing Synchro Dragon.

## Standard turns

- **SDQ + fodder** (Cyber Angel in hand, or Tour Guide + Djinn): discard SDQ (send Hymn, add Saffira), then
  banish it to Ritual Summon, because this spends no Ritual Spell from hand and puts Hymn in the GY.
- **Saffira + Hymn in hand**: Manju → Benten, then Hymn Tributing Benten, because Benten replaces itself and
  Hymn lands in the GY.
- **Tour Guide**: Tribute both for Saffira, or Rank 3 if they have Set cards to clear.
- **Neither half**: Pre-Preparation or Preparation of Rites.
- Activate searches before your Normal Summon, so you know which monster you need.

## General principles

- One Normal Summon: Tour Guide (fodder/Rank 3), Manju (search) or Witch (Lance), whichever the hand lacks.
- Going first: set up Saffira (ideally with a Djinn) and pass holding hand traps. Going second: hand traps on
  their turn, then Change of Heart / Rank 3 / Honest to break their board.
- Don't attack face-down monsters with your only Saffira if losing her loses the game.
- **Attack Position whenever Honest is a live out** (in hand, or her End Phase draw might find it): a Defense
  Saffira can't use it, and if they attack, Honest destroys their monster; a few hundred damage matters less.
  Honest boosts YOUR monster, so it works against monsters unaffected by other cards (e.g. Blackwing Full
  Armor Master).
