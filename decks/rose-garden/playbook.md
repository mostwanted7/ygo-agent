## How to use this playbook

These are principles, not scripts: read the board, and when a better play exists, make it and say why in `why`. Trust the engine's options over this text.

## Game plan

Synchro board-wipe deck. Lv3 Tuner + Lv4 Rose Dragon → Lv7 Black Rose Dragon (BRD); BRD + Lv3 Tuner → Lv10 Ruddy Rose Dragon. Both can wipe the field on Synchro Summon. Win by wiping, then hitting with Ruddy (3200) and Lv7s, usually in 3-5 turns. The best wipe is on the **opponent's Main Phase after they commit**, through Synchro Zone or Garden Rose Flora. Going first, a wipe hits nothing: end on Ruddy, on Synchro materials with Synchro Zone + Basal Set, or on Clear Wing. Grind with Roxrose's return, Basal/Maiden revives and Ruddy Rose Witch refilling BRD/Ruddy.

## Card roles

**Engine**
- **Roxrose** (Lv3 Dragon Tuner): on Normal/Special Summon searches a card naming BRD, once per turn. Returns from GY when your Rose Dragon/Plant Synchro is destroyed by battle or an **opponent's** effect; your own wipes don't count.
- **White Rose** (Lv4): Special Summons itself (once per turn) while you control a face-up Dragon or Plant Tuner (Roxrose, Red Rose, Rose Girl, Ruddy Rose Witch; Witch of the Black Rose is a Spellcaster, doesn't count). As material, mills a Lv4+ Plant (Mariña).
- **Red Rose** (Lv3 Tuner): as material, summons a non-Red Rose Dragon from hand or **Deck**, even when Ruddy's banish removed Red Rose in the same chain. Its Frozen Rose/Blooming add is only for BRD or a Plant Synchro, not Ruddy.
- **Lonefire**: Tribute a face-up Plant (itself ok) → Plant from Deck: Rose Girl (with White Rose → Periallis), Ruddy Rose Witch or Dandylion.
- **Ruddy Rose Witch**: Tribute → Witch of the Black Rose (Lv4 Tuner) + extra Normal Summon of her; the Witch draws only if she's your only card on the field. GY: banish → return a used BRD/Ruddy to the Extra Deck.
- **Dandylion** → two Lv1 tokens. **Rose Girl** SS herself when your face-up Plant goes to GY. **Rose Princess**: discard → Cloister; going second she SS herself as a Plant Tuner. **Spore** (once per Duel): banish Mariña → Lv9 Tuner.

**Bosses**
- **BRD**: summon wipe also kills itself and your Set cards; a revived BRD doesn't wipe. Banish a Plant → their Defense monster to Attack at 0 ATK.
- **Ruddy**: on Synchro Summon banishes **every** card in both GYs (no wipe if both empty), then, only if BRD or a Plant Synchro was material, yes/no to destroy all other cards. Quick: when opponent activates a destroy effect, Tribute it → negate, summon BRD from Extra Deck/GY (not banished).
- **Lv7s**: Moonlight (mandatory bounce, needs a Special Summoned target); Clear Wing (two negates with separate once-per-turn limits, each kill adds the victim's ATK; useful against Lv5+ threats); Periallis (Plant Synchro: enables Red Rose's add and Ruddy's wipe; revives a Lv5+ Plant in Defense).
- **Flora** (Lv5 Plant Tuner): Synchros on opponent's Main Phase: + Rose Token → BRD, + Maiden → Ruddy, + Lv3 + Lv1 → Trishula. Its Field-destroy locks you to Synchros. **Maiden**: on SS adds Black Garden; GY: banish → revive a Rose Dragon or Dragon Synchro. **Trishula** needs **two** non-Tuners. Coral, Omega: generic removal.

**Spells/Traps**
- **Basal**: summons a Rose Dragon from hand/GY in Defense (works on empty field; Roxrose still searches). If the **Set** card is destroyed, your own wipe included, revives BRD or a card naming it from GY **or banishment**. Each effect once per turn.
- **Synchro Zone**: face-up, **no non-Synchro can attack, yours too**. On their Main Phase send it → Synchro Summon; flip it first in Draw/Standby. Face-up, it revives your non-Tuner Synchro sent to GY (e.g. Ruddy Tributed for its negate) as a Tuner.
- **Cloister**: no monsters → summon a Rose Dragon/Plant from hand. **Black Garden** halves summoned monsters (yours too) and gives the summoner's opponent a Rose Token: activate after your summons.
- **Frozen Rose** (one per turn): non-Plant → Lv4- Plant, or Plant → draw 2 discard 1; best chained to your own BRD wipe sending that BRD. **Miracle Fertilizer** revives a Plant only if you haven't Normal Summoned.

## Situational considerations

- **Count Tuners**: one per Synchro. Roxrose + Red Rose can't Synchro together; add White Rose or Dandylion.
- **Roxrose search**: Red Rose if you can still summon it, else Basal (GY Roxrose becomes Ruddy's second Tuner).
- **Save the Normal Summon** for Red Rose, Rose Girl or a Witch: Basal/Cloister make the first body, White Rose summons itself.
- **Set Basal before a wipe only if GY or banishment will hold BRD or a card naming it**, because it then returns an extra attacker; else keep it.
- **Chain order**: when the wipe triggers with Red Rose's or Dandylion's summon, summon CL1, wipe CL2 so the new monsters survive.
- **Consider the opponent-turn wipe** (Lv3 Tuner + Lv4 face-up, Synchro Zone + Basal Set); vs removal-heavy decks Ruddy is the safer wall. Flip Zone in Draw/Standby only when face-up material is ready; otherwise keep it Set and flip at their Battle Phase or not at all, because face-up it stops your non-Synchros and draws removal.
- **Decline turn-1 Ruddy's banish**: it loses the GY BRD that Ruddy's negate and Maiden revive.
- **On your turn prefer Ruddy's wipe over BRD's**, because Ruddy survives. Keep BRD in the Extra Deck when it would wipe something.
- **Don't spend Ruddy's negate on a trade you like** (their boss destroying itself plus a spare card of yours).
- **Tribute Ruddy Rose Witch before** Cloister, Black Garden or setting cards.
- **Extra Deck**: 2 Ruddy, 2 BRD; Ruddy Rose Witch's GY effect refills it.

## Lethal check

- Attackers: Ruddy 3200, Clear Wing 2500, BRD/other Lv7 2400, Periallis 1600 + 400 per other Plant. GY Mariña = 2800 via Miracle Fertilizer (only before Normal Summon; it then bans it). Lv7+ Synchros +1000 on a right Cloister guess.
- A second Lv7 next to a Synchro: NS Roxrose (Basal) → Basal revives a Lv4 Rose Dragon → BRD, decline wipe.
- Small bodies: after Ruddy clears their only monster, a Normal Summoned Rose Lover/Witch (800) or White Rose (1200) hits directly; summon in Main Phase 1.
- Blockers: Ruddy's wipe (survives); BRD makes a Defense monster 0 ATK; Splendid Rose halves one and attacks twice.
- Don't wipe when your face-up Synchros already win: BRD's wipe kills them and your Set cards.
- Face-up Synchro Zone (either side): count only Synchros.
- Attack first with what you can afford to lose to a Set card; lethal piece last.
- Worked example: Ruddy 3200 + Basal-revived BRD 2400 + Red Rose → Roxrose 1600 = 7200.

## Going second / breaking boards

- First choice: Ruddy wipe with Basal Set: BRD (decline wipe) + Lv3 Tuner → Ruddy. With Red Rose as Tuner, it pulls Roxrose after the wipe and Basal revives banished BRD.
- One Synchro's worth: BRD's wipe still resets; chain Frozen Rose sending BRD, Set Basal to get it back. Rebuild in Main Phase 2 with Basal/Cloister.
- Rose Princess: free Tuner when only they have monsters. Moonlight bounces a Special Summoned boss. Trishula hits hand, field, GY.

## Example sequences

- **Ruddy wipe going second**: Basal → Roxrose (Basal) → White Rose SS → BRD no wipe → NS Red Rose → Set Basal → Ruddy (Red Rose CL1, wipe CL2) → Roxrose from Deck, Basal revives BRD → 7200.
- **Lonefire → Periallis → Ruddy**: Terraforming → Cloister → Lonefire, Tribute → Rose Girl → White Rose SS → Periallis (mill Mariña) → revive Mariña, Rose Girl to hand → NS Rose Girl → Ruddy wipe, leaving Mariña in the GY for later revival.
- **Clear Wing wall going first**: Basal → Roxrose (Blue Rose) → NS Blue Rose → Clear Wing, Set Frozen Rose.
